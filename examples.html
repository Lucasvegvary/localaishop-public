How to read these

Each example names a time-drain, then the kind of tool that handles it. Plenty of them are plain software with no AI in it, and some may already be sitting unused inside software you pay for. I'd pick whatever is boring, proven and fits how you already work. In every one, the tool does the repeat work and a person stays in the loop: you, or whoever you trust with the office.
