How North Bay plumbers and landscapers stand with the state license board

We read the California Contractors State License Board's public license file of November 6, 2026 for every plumbing (C-36) and landscaping (C-27) license in Marin, Napa, Solano and Sonoma counties, and sorted each one by what the state's own record shows · counts only · no names, ever · published by The Local AI Shop.

What counts against a license: a suspension, probation or pending case on its status; a pending legal action; a disciplinary bond; or no workers' compensation on file when the business is not exempt. A business with no employees can be legally exempt from workers' comp, and that does not count against it. A renewal the state has received but not yet processed does not count against it either.

What this does not measure: the quality of anyone's work, their reviews, or how they answer the phone.
