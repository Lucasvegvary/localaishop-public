Send me a shop. Get $200 toward your own.

You know owners who are buried in the office side of the work: missed calls, estimates that never get followed up, paperwork after dinner. I help owner-run trade and home-service shops get those hours back with AI, and I build simple websites for shops that don't have one.

For every shop you send that becomes a paying client, you get a $200 credit toward any Local AI Shop service. No limit on how many. One limit on use: credits never take a Second Shift Study below $499.

How it works

  1. Tell the owner about The Local AI Shop.
  2. Text or email me their name, their business, their phone number, and your name. Or have them say your name when they call or book.
  3. Your $200 credit is added, and I email you, once their first payment has cleared and: for a Second Shift Study, 60 days have passed since their walkthrough with no refund request; for a website, that first payment is not refunded.

  3. Credits apply to any Local AI Shop service, stack with each other, are good for 12 months from the date they are added, and have no cash value. Rewards are account credit only. The floor: no mix of credits and discounts, however many you have earned, can take a Second Shift Study below $499. Credit that would take it lower is not applied to the Study; it stays on your account for other services until it expires.

  5. I only take on shops I believe I can help. A referral is never a promise that I will take the work.
