05 · 4-Day Quick-Start Plan

Ten minutes a day. No freezing. No overwhelm.

Day 1

Book two field service demos

Pick two apps built for trades and book both. In the notes, ask what six seats cost all in, whether it texts customers both ways, and whether it pushes invoices to your bookkeeping.

≈ 10 min

Day 2

Switch on missed-call text-back

Point the shop line at it, then call the shop from the truck to watch the text arrive. This one can start working the same afternoon.

≈ 10 min

Day 3

Put the receipt app on two phones

Yours and Kim's. Photograph one supply house receipt at the counter and let it file itself. Then stop emptying the truck door into a shoebox.

≈ 10 min

Day 4

Start the inbox trial

Connect the shop inbox and then leave it alone for a week. Do not sort anything by hand while it is still learning what matters.

≈ 10 min

Do only this, and by Thursday, August 27 the text-back, the receipt app, and the inbox tool are all running: 3.0 of the 10.0 hours, in week one. The field service app is a migration, not a sign-up, so its 7.0 hours arrive after the cutover date you set with the vendor, not this week.
