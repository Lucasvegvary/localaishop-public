05 · 4-Day Quick-Start Plan

Ten minutes a day, one step at a time.

Day 1

Book the Tekmetric demo

There is no free trial, so the demo is your look. Ask to see only the screens you would use: estimates and parts ordering with your own suppliers, and the tech side on a phone (not a tablet). Then ask what Accounting Link and its setup fee cost, and get month-to-month in writing.

≈ 10 min

Day 2

List your parts suppliers

Write down the two or three parts houses you price from and which logins they need, so onboarding can connect them.

≈ 10 min

Day 3

Call Denise

Tell her Accounting Link would post your closed tickets into QuickBooks, and ask her to sit in on its setup call.

≈ 10 min

Day 4

Pick your switch day

Choose a day in a slow week. From that day every ticket and every appointment goes into Tekmetric: it becomes your one appointment book, and the paper book and the carbonless pads retire.

≈ 10 min

Do only this and by Saturday, October 10, your demo is booked, your suppliers are listed, Denise is in, and your switch day is picked. Tekmetric is a switch from paper, not a sign-up, so its 4.1 hours and Accounting Link's 2.5 hours land after the switch day you pick on Day 4, not in week one.
