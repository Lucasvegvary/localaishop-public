Before you book

Questions

Why did you mail me a letter?
So it can be about your shop and nobody else's. Each letter starts from notes on what your business already shows the public, mostly your own website. AI tools help gather those notes, and a competent human in the loop checks them before anything goes in the mail. A letter also waits until you have a minute. If I follow up with a call, say stop and I'll stop. And if anything in the letter is wrong, you know exactly whose name is on it.
Is the free 15 minutes a sales call?
It is a working conversation, not a generic demo. I look at how you run things and give you one specific idea you can keep. At the end, if the full Study looks useful, I'll ask whether you want it. The price and guarantee are already printed on this page.
We already use ChatGPT. Do we still need this?
Good, you've already tried one tool. The assessment looks at the office-side work around it: email, scheduling, quotes, follow-up, and the pile that sits and waits for you. In the free 15 minutes, I look at one workflow with you and give you one practical idea to keep.
What if you can't find 5 hours in my business?
You still receive your full Shift Sheet and your walkthrough, and I refund the $999. I count only honest, non-duplicative hours, starting from time estimates you confirm out loud on your discovery call, and I never pad the numbers to dodge the refund. The full wording and its conditions are in the guarantee above and on my terms page.
When do I get my Shift Sheet?
Before your walkthrough call. The walkthrough is scheduled when you book the Study, and your Shift Sheet lands in your inbox ahead of it, so you can read it first and bring questions.
Aren't we too small for this?
Small is exactly who this is for. I work with owner-run home and trade service businesses across Napa, Solano and Sonoma counties, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, remodeling, landscaping, cleaning, pest control and auto repair, roughly 2 to 20 people. Big companies have IT departments; you've got you. Five hours a week back matters more at your size, not less.
Is there anyone you don't work with?
Yes, and I'd rather say it here than on the fourth call. I don't take medical, dental, chiropractic, veterinary or therapy practices yet. Those carry patient records, and I don't have the compliance lane for them. It would be wrong to pretend otherwise. If that's your business, I'll tell you on the first call, not the fourth.
I don't really trust AI. Why should I do this?
If AI has felt like it wasn't built for a business like yours, that's not you being behind. It has mostly been sold in a language from another planet, by people who have never run a shop. Healthy instinct: a lot of what's out there is hype. The job is filtering that noise down to as few tools as the work needs: boring, proven, and worth your money. A lot of what I recommend is plain software with no AI in it at all. Whatever fixes the problem. Nothing experimental goes near your business. The AI is the wrench, not the job. You're not buying artificial intelligence. You're buying your Tuesday nights back.
Will AI end up running my business?
No. You will. My job is to take the repeat work off your plate, not to take you out of the loop. A reminder text can go out without you, but which jobs you take and what you promise a customer stay your call. Anything I build goes in your accounts: you own every login, and every piece has an off switch you control. Keeping a human in the loop is the responsible, ethical way to work with AI. Same goes for me: AI speeds up my research, and every recommendation in your report is mine to answer for.
Do you record the calls? What happens to my information?
The 45-minute discovery call is recorded, but only if you say yes. I ask out loud at the start. Here's exactly what happens to it: the recording is transcribed and analyzed with AI software to draft your report (that software receives your call content only to do that job), and a human reviews every page before it ships. The recording is deleted 30 days after your report is delivered. The working transcript is deleted after 90 days. Your final report is deleted after 12 months, unless you ask me to keep it longer. Want anything gone sooner? One email to lucas@localaishop.com, any time. Nothing you say is sold, and nothing trains AI models unless you separately choose to allow a de-identified version for my internal training (a completely optional checkbox; saying no changes nothing). Full details in my privacy policy.
Which tools do you push? Are you selling software?
I'm not a software company, and no tool pays my bills; the Study fee is my product. I favor boring, proven, off-the-shelf tools you could buy on your own tomorrow, including plain software with no AI when it fits better.
