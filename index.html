Small is exactly who this is for. Is there anyone you don't work with?
Yes, and I'd rather say it here than on the fourth call. I don't take medical, dental, chiropractic, veterinary or therapy practices yet. Those carry patient records, and I don't have the compliance lane for them. It would be wrong to pretend otherwise. If that's your business, I'll tell you on the first call, not the fourth.
I don't really trust AI. Why should I do this?
You shouldn't trust it blindly, and I don't either. A lot of what's sold to small businesses is hype, and the tools keep changing. Keeping up with AI is what I do, so you don't have to. I only recommend tools that have held up, each with the reason it fits your business. Nothing goes on the list unless you hear the math and say yes out loud. The AI is the wrench, not the job. You're not buying artificial intelligence. You're buying your Tuesday nights back.
Plenty of AI companies say they can fix the same problems. What makes you different?
My commitment to every owner I work with: I'll always underpromise and overdeliver. My reputation matters, and around here a name travels by word of mouth. Integrity comes first in how I run this shop. Whatever you hire me for, my goal is the same: improve how your business runs as much as I possibly can.
Will AI end up running my business?
No. You will. My job is to take the repeat work off your plate, not to take you out of the loop. A reminder text can go out without you, but which jobs you take and what you promise a customer stay your call. Anything I build goes in your accounts: you own every login, and every piece has an off switch you control. Keeping a human in the loop is the responsible, ethical way to work with AI. Same goes for me: AI speeds up my research, and every recommendation in your report is mine to answer for.
